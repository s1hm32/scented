Our Story

Crafted for the quiet moments

Lumina Scents began with a simple idea: that scent can hold memory, calm the mind, and turn ordinary rooms into sanctuaries. Every candle is hand-poured in small batches using clean-burning soy wax and cotton wicks. Our fragrances are layered with care — top notes that greet you, heart notes that linger, and base notes that stay with you long after.

We believe luxury should feel personal. Whether you are lighting a candle for a quiet evening or choosing a signature scent for your day, we hope our collection becomes part of your ritual.
